User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This page explains how users of the Postiz instance at postiz.aissistente.com.br can request deletion of data connected through Meta products such as Facebook and Instagram.

Remove access from Meta

You can revoke this app's access from your Meta account at any time:

  1. Open Facebook Settings and privacy.
  2. Go to Settings, then Apps and Websites.
  3. Find the app connected to this Postiz instance.
  4. Click Remove to disconnect it.

Request deletion from this Postiz instance

To request deletion of stored account data, connected social account tokens, profile identifiers, scheduled posts, media, and related data held by this Postiz instance, email contato@aissistente.com.br from the email address associated with your account.

Use the subject line Postiz data deletion request and include the email address used in Postiz plus the social account or page you want removed. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing the request.

Processing time

Verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law or for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Data that is aggregated or anonymized so that it cannot identify a user may be retained.

Important note

Removing this app or deleting data from Postiz does not delete data stored by Meta. To manage or delete data held by Facebook or Instagram, use your Meta account settings.
