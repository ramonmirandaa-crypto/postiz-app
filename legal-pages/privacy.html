Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Postiz instance available at postiz.aissistente.com.br.

Data we process

We process account information, connected social account identifiers, OAuth tokens, scheduled post content, media, analytics, and technical logs needed to provide social media scheduling and publishing features.

How we use data

We use data to authenticate users, connect social channels, schedule and publish content, show analytics, troubleshoot service issues, and keep the service secure.

Third-party platforms

When you connect Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, or another platform, we use the access granted by that platform only to provide the features you request.

Deletion requests

To request deletion of your data, see User Data Deletion Instructions or email contato@aissistente.com.br.
