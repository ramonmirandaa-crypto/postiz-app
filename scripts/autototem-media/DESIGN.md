# AutoTotem Social Media Style

## Style Prompt

Marketing assets should follow the visual language of `autototem.com.br`: clean SaaS presentation, dark navy hero surfaces, soft translucent cards, emerald action highlights, subtle grid texture, and real tablet/totem product mockups. The mood is operational, modern, and practical rather than playful or stock-photo heavy.

## Colors

- `#061126`: primary brand ink, dark hero background, logo tile
- `#00C98B`: primary action green, dot accent, CTA pills, progress bars
- `#F3F7F6`: light page background
- `#D7E5EA`: muted text on dark backgrounds
- `#45556C`: muted text on light backgrounds
- `#0A4B4B`: dark teal organic background shapes

## Typography

- Use Inter-like grotesk sizing and rhythm. On the server, render with `DejaVu Sans` as the available fallback.
- Use monospace only for operational labels and metrics, matching the site's product/data language.

## Motion

- Keep transitions smooth and minimal: fade, slight vertical drift, and subtle card motion.
- Do not use loud wipes, 3D spins, heavy gradients, or decorative bokeh.

## What Not To Do

- Do not return to the old flat royal-blue icon-only style.
- Do not use generic stock restaurant photos.
- Do not overcrowd the frame with too many benefits at once.
- Do not use random colors outside the site palette.
